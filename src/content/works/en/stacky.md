## Overview

"I want to see what I copied a moment ago." Stacky is a clipboard history and snippet app where copied text quietly stacks up the moment you open it. For iPhone and Mac.

## What it does

- **Auto capture**: copy in any app, open Stacky, and it is in the history
- **Save & copy**: one action from the share sheet saves the text and copies it
- **Back tap**: assign "save clip" to the iPhone back tap
- **Snippets**: keep frequently used text ready to paste

## Technical notes

- Everything stays on the device; nothing is sent to a server, and there are no ads
